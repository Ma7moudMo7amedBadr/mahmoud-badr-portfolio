Dec 2025 · Computer Vision

Smart Document Scanner App

Transforming skewed, low-quality images into clean, shareable PDFs using computer vision.

Problem

Manual scanning and mobile photos often produce skewed, noisy documents that are hard to read, store, and share professionally.

Solution

Built a Streamlit-based app using Python and OpenCV (Canny edge detection and perspective transformation) to automatically detect document boundaries and correct perspective before exporting to PDF.

Impact

Produces consistently clean, aligned PDFs from casual photos, reducing manual correction time and improving the perceived quality of shared documents.
